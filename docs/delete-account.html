Delete your CaliSync account

You can permanently delete your account and its associated data.

Delete in the app

Sign in, open Profile → Settings → Delete account, then confirm. Settings are also available from the expired-plan screen. This removes your account from the active service immediately.

Request deletion without the app

Email dipaoloigor@gmail.com from the email address used for your account, with the subject “CaliSync account deletion”. State that you want your account and associated data deleted.

Prepare deletion request

This opens your email application. The request is submitted only when you send the email. Support verifies ownership before deleting data; if you cannot access your account email, contact the same address for help. Do not send your password.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account, profile, uploaded profile photos and notification registrations.
  • Your trainee workout plans and exercises, progress, feedback and replies, weight history, maximum tests and their private notes, and payment records.
  • Your trainer/admin role and trainer–trainee assignments, if applicable.

If you are a trainer, deleting your account does not delete other users' accounts or their workout history. Shared workout days are retained only when another user's plan still uses them.

Deletion is irreversible. Copies previously downloaded by other users cannot be removed from their devices. Infrastructure backups may retain historical copies until their configured retention period expires; these are not available through the active app. Contact support for details about backup retention.
