CaliSync Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026

CaliSync (also displayed as Calisthenics) helps trainees follow workout programs and communicate with their trainers. Igor Maria Di Paolo operates the app. For privacy questions and requests, contact dipaoloigor@gmail.com.

This policy covers the app, its Supabase backend, push notifications, and support requests. It describes the current service, including its access limitations. We do not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising.

Who can access your information

  • You: your profile, programs, recorded progress, feedback/replies and payment information are available through your account.
  • Assigned trainers: trainers assigned to your account can access profile details, body measurements and history, programs, exercise records, maximum tests, feedback/replies and payment records to provide coaching. More than one trainer can be assigned; feedback is accessible to assigned trainers and is not a private conversation exclusively with one named trainer.
  • App administrators: admins manage user roles, suspension and trainer assignments. Current backend permissions also allow broad access to profiles, feedback, plans, progress, weights and payments. Trainer-only maximum-test notes have no admin-role access, but an admin who is also an assigned trainer can access them as a trainer. Do not assume all coaching information is hidden from administrators.
  • Technical operators and providers: authorized backend operators and service credentials can access server-stored data to run the service. Database access rules do not prevent privileged operators from reading plaintext records.

Workout-day access is restricted to the trainee, assigned trainers and administrators. Trainers may also access their own newly created days before linking them to a plan. A trainer role alone does not grant access to other trainees' workout days.

Profile-photo visibility: uploaded avatars use public storage URLs. Anyone who has a photo URL may be able to view that image without signing in. Do not upload sensitive documents or confidential images as your profile photo.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or necessary to investigate misuse and protect the service. Your trainer may maintain their own records outside CaliSync; contact them separately about those records.

Hosting and service providers

Supabase hosts authentication, database records, profile-photo storage and backend functions. Google Firebase provides push registration and message delivery; Apple delivers iOS notifications through APNs. Support emails are handled through the support mailbox provider. Visiting these public policy pages also sends ordinary web-request information to GitHub Pages, our website host.

Providers may process data in countries outside your country of residence, depending on service configuration and their infrastructure. Relevant provider information: Supabase privacy, Firebase privacy and security, Apple privacy, and GitHub privacy.

Push notifications and message previews

On supported Android/iOS installations, Firebase initializes when the app starts. When a signed-in user allows notifications, the app obtains a push token and records it in Supabase with the account ID and platform. Firebase also processes installation identifiers and technical delivery data under its own service terms.

When feedback is submitted or answered, a plan or coach tip changes, or payment status is updated, the app sends an event type and record ID to a backend function. That function validates the sender, reads the corresponding database records, resolves recipients and their push tokens, then sends a notification payload to Firebase. Firebase routes Apple-device messages through APNs.

The current payload can contain personal content: a trainee name, an excerpt of feedback or a trainer reply, a coach tip, a plan title, or paid/unpaid status. Payloads also include the event type and an app-navigation route. Google and Apple delivery services process these payloads, and previews can appear on the lock screen or in the app. Notifications are not end-to-end encrypted.

You can turn off notifications or lock-screen previews in your device settings. Refusing notification permission does not delete stored coaching records. Logout attempts to unregister the current device token; account deletion removes that account's backend token records. Invalid/unregistered tokens reported by Firebase are removed by the sending function. Provider-side identifiers and queued messages follow the provider's retention rules.

Security

The app communicates with its backend over HTTPS and uses authenticated sessions and database access rules. Personal records are readable by the backend; they are not protected by client-side end-to-end encryption. Public photo URLs, administrator access and notification previews have the limitations described above. Do not include information in notes or messages that you do not want the authorized recipients or technical operators to access.

Retention and deletion

Active account, coaching, body-measurement, feedback and payment records are kept to provide your account and history until you delete those records through available features or delete your account. The app has no automatic inactivity-deletion schedule. Device preferences and timer state remain locally until cleared, removed with the app, or cleared by the account-deletion flow.

Use Profile → Settings → Delete account to permanently remove your sign-in account and associated active-service records. Account controls remain available when training access is unpaid. You can also request deletion without the app by emailing support from your account email. Support verifies ownership before completing an email request.

Successful in-app deletion removes authentication, your profile and uploaded avatars, trainee plans and unshared workout days/exercises, progress records, feedback/replies, weight history, maximum tests and associated private notes, payment records, exercise unlocks, notification registrations, roles and assignments. The app clears locally cached photo URLs and timer data and signs you out. Some photos may be removed before a database deletion error; retry or contact support if deletion reports a failure.

Deleting a trainer account removes its role and assignments; it does not delete other users' accounts or their workout history. Workout days still used by another user's plan are retained. Downloaded copies, screenshots and independent records held by recipients cannot be erased by the app.

Deletion from the active service does not immediately erase historical infrastructure backups, provider operational logs, provider-side installation data or support email. Their retention follows the actual provider/project configuration and applicable obligations. There is currently no app-enforced fixed retention period for support email. Contact support for the current backup/log retention configuration and to request removal of correspondence where applicable. We do not promise a fixed purge deadline that the service does not enforce.

Your choices and requests

You can edit profile information, choose whether to upload a photo, adjust device permissions, sign out and delete your account. Contact dipaoloigor@gmail.com to request access, a copy, correction, restriction or deletion of data that you cannot manage in the app. We verify identity before disclosing information or acting on account requests.

We process account and coaching data to provide the service you request, operational information to maintain security and reliability, and optional device permissions where you authorize them. Depending on your jurisdiction and the data involved, additional consent or other legal requirements may apply, particularly to health information. Where applicable, you may withdraw consent, object to processing or contact your data-protection authority. This policy does not waive those rights.

The app is not designed to solicit children's personal information. If a child has supplied data without appropriate authorization, contact support so we can review and address it.

Changes and contact

We update the effective date when this policy changes. Material changes affecting how personal data is used or shared will be communicated through an appropriate service channel. Questions about your trainer's separate activities should also be directed to that trainer.
